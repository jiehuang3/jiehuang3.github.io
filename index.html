Jie HUANG

PhD Candidate in Health Informatics at the University of Melbourne, in a joint program with the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. I model longitudinal electronic health records with language models — from representation and explainability to privacy-preserving synthetic patient data generation with agentic AI.

Email LinkedIn Scholar GitHub

Research Interests

// how longitudinal clinical data should be represented, explained, and generated

REPRESENTATION

EHR Serialization & Foundation Models

How longitudinal structured EHRs should be serialized as text for clinical language models, and how representation choices shape downstream prediction.

EXPLAINABILITY

Explainable Clinical Prediction

Token-level explainability for EHR foundation models — revealing which parts of a patient's history drive model predictions.

GENERATION

Synthetic Patient Data

Privacy-preserving generation of synthetic longitudinal patient trajectories, and evaluation frameworks for clinical plausibility.

AGENTS

Agentic AI for Clinical Data

Agentic systems (DSPy / LangGraph) for patient-data modelling: structuring raw clinical records for agent consumption and designing clinical tools for agents.
